# mfact v2030.1.1 — PRD / ARD

**Repository:** `seanchatmangpt/mfact`  
**Ecosystem role:** formal-proof / certification  
**Depends on:** `ggen`  
**Release:** Chatman Ecosystem v2030.1.1  
**Standing conferred:** `UNKNOWN`.

## Product requirement

`mfact` SHALL be the mathematical certification boundary of the Chatman Ecosystem. It exists to convert selected semantic and implementation claims into machine-checkable obligations, admit what is proved, preserve what remains merely stated, and issue evidence without deciding business policy or granting runtime authority.

The v2030.1.1 product law remains:

> **ggen renders; Lean admits; mfact certifies.**

## Required capabilities

1. Accept exact semantic/source identities and manufacture formal projections only through declared ggen sources/templates.
2. Maintain explicit states such as STATED, PROVEN, UNSUPPORTED, BLOCKED, and applicable ecosystem standing without prose-based promotion.
3. Certify theorem and artifact identity through ledgered, replayable manifests.
4. Verify theorem dependency/axiom status and prevent `sorry`/unadmitted assumptions from masquerading as proof.
5. Establish formal/runtime correspondence when a product claim depends on generated/extracted runtime objects.
6. Support countermodels and typed refusals as first-class evidence.
7. Keep core certified release identities immutable; post-release witnesses SHALL NOT recursively rewrite the identity they witness.
8. Provide exact, content-addressed certification artifacts consumable by AutoFDE, Praxis, FDEGym, and release governance.

## Architecture requirements

```text
semantic law / source declaration
      -> ggen formal projection
      -> Lean admission
      -> axiom + correspondence audit
      -> mfact certification
      -> immutable manifest / receipt
```

Formal proof SHALL be used where the required claim needs it; executable verification remains appropriate for claims about real external behavior. Proof text or a theorem declaration confers no actuation authority.

No ledgered/generated formal artifact may be hand-edited as a final solution. Changes flow through source declarations/templates and the repository's manufacturing recipes.

## Acceptance for v2030.1.1

- every PROVEN claim is bound to an exact admitted theorem and mechanical axiom audit;
- generated formal surfaces replay from their ledgered source inputs;
- runtime correspondence claims reference the actual extracted/generated declaration rather than a same-shaped surrogate;
- tampered or orphan artifacts are refused;
- certification manifests are content-addressed and reproducible;
- negative/countermodel evidence prevents invalid status promotion;
- downstream consumers can verify a certificate without trusting narrative prose;
- no proof or certificate object can directly authorize external DO.

## Falsifiers

The architecture is falsified if a status literal can promote STATED to PROVEN without a mechanical check; if generated theorem artifacts can diverge from source without detection; if a correspondence theorem never binds the runtime object it claims to correspond to; or if certification implicitly becomes policy selection or actuation.

## Ecosystem contract

`mfact` certifies bounded mathematical/formal claims. It does not decide which architecture to choose and does not execute production actions. `ggen` owns projection manufacture; Lean owns theorem admission; runtime systems own observed execution; receipts compose these independent evidence classes.
